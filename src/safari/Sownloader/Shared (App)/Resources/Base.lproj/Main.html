Sownloader automatically adds a Sownloader button to Smule performance pages, making it simple to save your favorite recordings in just a few clicks.

How to enable the extension

1. Open settings

2. Go to Apps

3. Go to Safari

4. Go to Extensions

5. Select Sownloader

6. Enable the extension and give permissions for smule.com

How to Use

  1. Enable the extension as described above
  2. Open any performance in Safari on Smule.com
  3. Wait for the Sownloader button to appear on the bottom right
  4. Click the button
  5. Choose whether you want to download the audio or video
  6. Your media can be found within the Apple Files app
